# MassLens Google Play ASO Pack

Research date: 15 August 2026

## Recommended positioning

MassLens should own the honest hybrid position in this niche:

- Food & macros scan: estimated weight ranges for visible plate components,
  with approximate calories and macros calculated from matched USDA data.
- AI photo scan: an approximate weight range, visual size estimate, and explicit assumptions.
- Compatible Bluetooth scale: a real reading from an external device implementing the Bluetooth SIG Weight Scale standard.
- Touch scale: an interactive simulator, clearly disclosed as not being a physical weighing tool.

This avoids the biggest trust problem in competing “phone scale” listings: implying that a touchscreen can physically weigh an object. No metadata can guarantee a number-one ranking. The practical goal is to maximize qualified search relevance and conversion without creating misleading expectations, bad reviews, or policy risk.

cluster: `AI food scale`, `food weight scale app`, `kitchen scale app`, `food
weight estimator`, `estimate food weight from photo`, and `ingredient weight in
grams`. Use each idea only where it describes real behavior. Avoid `Omron`,
other competitor trademarks, broad irrelevant terms such as `measure`, and
misleading precision terms such as `milligram scale`. Keyword repetition should
read naturally, not like a search-term block.

### Long description

```text
Estimate food weight, calories and macros from a plate photo, estimate an everyday object’s likely weight, or connect a compatible Bluetooth scale for a real live reading.

MassLens brings an AI food scale, object weight estimator, compatible Bluetooth scale reader, and clearly labeled touch simulator together in one app.

AI OBJECT WEIGHT SCAN
• Take a photo or choose one from your gallery.
• Add an optional side view to give the AI better shape and volume clues.
• Receive an approximate weight range in grams, kilograms, ounces, or pounds.
• See an AI visual estimate of length × width × height.
• Review the material, construction, fill, scale, and uncertainty assumptions behind the result.
• Save estimates to private on-device history.

AI FOOD SCALE & MACRO ESTIMATOR
• Choose Food & macros, then photograph one whole plate or select an image from your gallery.
• Add an optional second angle and, when known, the plate or bowl diameter for better visual context.
• Review an estimated weight range for each visible food and for the plate total.
• See approximate calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat calculated from matched USDA FoodData Central records.
• Correct a portion weight or remove a false detection and review the recalculated total.
• See “partial total” when any food has no suitable nutrition match.
• Save the result to private on-device history and share a clearly labeled estimate.

MassLens works as an AI food scale and food weight estimator: estimate food weight from a photo, review an ingredient weight in grams, and see the uncertainty before using the result. Like any food weight scale app or kitchen scale app based on images, it provides estimates—not physical readings.

BLUETOOTH WEIGHT SCALE
• Connect a compatible Bluetooth Low Energy scale.
• View the live physical reading in a clean digital display.
• Keep real scale readings separate from AI estimates and simulator values.

TOUCH SCALE SIMULATOR
• Explore a realistic digital-scale interface with tare and unit controls.
• Use it for demonstration and interaction only—it does not weigh objects through the phone screen.

USEFUL WHEN AN APPROXIMATE RANGE IS ENOUGH
Use Object Scan for early planning around household objects, coffee, parcels, luggage, crafts, and everyday items. A clear photo, neutral background, full-object framing, and optional side view can improve the visual estimate.

BLUETOOTH COMPATIBILITY
An external scale must expose Bluetooth SIG Weight Scale Service 0x181D and Weight Measurement characteristic 0x2A9D. Compatibility varies by model, region, and firmware; verify those UUIDs before purchase. MassLens does not certify or endorse a particular scale.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
MassLens does not turn your phone or touchscreen into a physical weighing scale. Touch Scale is an interactive simulator and cannot measure real mass. AI scans analyze photos to provide approximate AI-generated weight ranges, visual dimensions, food labels, and assumptions; results may be wrong and vary with lighting, angle, material, framing, hidden ingredients, portion shape, and image quality. Calories and macros are approximate calculations based on estimated portions and matched USDA records. They are not medical advice, dietary advice, or a medical-device reading. Only readings received from a compatible external Bluetooth scale are physical measurements. Many consumer smart scales use proprietary protocols and will not connect even if they appear in Android Bluetooth settings. Never place objects on the phone screen. For medical dosing, regulated trade, commercial transactions, shipping charges, jewelry valuation, laboratory work, allergy decisions, or any safety-critical decision, use an appropriate certified tool and qualified professional.
```

## Default-listing release strategy

Use the default listing above for this release. Do not rename the app or create a
keyword-targeted custom store listing. Screenshots must show the actual Food &
macros flow, approximate ranges, USDA-based nutrition, and the partial-total
state. Never show food or another object resting on the phone as if screen
pressure were weighing it.

## Store experiment priorities

1. Keep `MassLens: AI Weight Scale` fixed and test the first screenshot: a Food
   Scale result versus the hybrid AI + Bluetooth promise.
2. Test short-description or screenshot changes through default store-listing
   experiments, one variable at a time.
3. Localize screenshots and captions for English, French, and Spanish.
4. Ask for an honest rating after a successful result or Bluetooth session—not
   on first launch before the user receives value.
5. Track keyword impressions/ranks, store-listing acquisition, install
   conversion, first Food Scale completion, day-7 retention, rating, uninstall
   rate, food-mode usage, latency, and AI cost together.

## Google Play release compliance

- Select **Nutrition and Weight Management** in the Play Console Health
  declaration. Do not position MassLens as health coaching, a nutrition diary,
  a medical device, or a source of dietary recommendations.
- Supply the public HTTPS policy through `MASSLENS_PRIVACY_URL`, show the same
  link in Settings and this store listing, and block release builds when it is
  absent or invalid.
- Explain photo processing by the Worker/OpenAI, normalized text-only food
  queries to USDA, private on-device history, and deletion in the privacy policy
  and Data safety answers. USDA must never receive photos or identifiers.
- Verify the in-app and listing disclaimer: approximate results only; not a
  medical device or dietary advice.
